Apple Inc.
Apple Inc. is a global technology powerhouse headquartered in Cupertino, California, that has fundamentally reshaped the landscape of modern personal electronics. Founded on April 1, 1976, by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, the company rose from a garage-based startup to become the first U.S. company to reach a $3 trillion market valuation. Apple is renowned for its seamless integration of hardware, software, and services, creating a "walled garden" ecosystem that prioritizes user experience, minimalist design, and premium branding. Its product history is marked by revolutionary milestones, including the Macintosh (1984), which popularized the graphical user interface; the iPod (2001), which transformed the music industry; and the iPhone (2007), which redefined the smartphone. Today, under the leadership of CEO Tim Cook, the company continues to dominate through the iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and a growing Services sector that includes the App Store, Apple Music, and iCloud.
